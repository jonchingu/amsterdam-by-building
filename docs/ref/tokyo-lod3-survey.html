LOD3 in Tokyo's newer models covers one district

I read every building in the 2025 per-ward files for Chiyoda, Chūō, Minato, Shinjuku and Shibuya, plus Minato's 2023 release. Detail above LOD2 exists only around Toranomon Hills. None of the files carry a construction year, and the famous Omotesando and Aoyama landmarks are still plain blocks.

Toranomon in 3D

The government's textured LOD3 tiles, the same ones PLATEAU VIEW streams, cut down to the 11 tiles that hold LOD3 buildings (977 buildings, 102 of them LOD3). Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan, or fly it like a drone with the pad or the keys below. Tap a building for its record. PLATEAU VIEW also has a finer BIM model of Toranomon Hills that isn't published for download.
